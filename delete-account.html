Delete your RepRise account

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Option 1: delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open RepRise and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Backup & Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your account and every cloud backup copy. It cannot be undone.

Option 2: ask us to delete it

If you no longer have the app, email keerthivarma2507@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign up, with the subject Delete my RepRise account. We will delete your account and backup and reply to confirm, within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and sign-in).
  • Your cloud backup, including all older copies.

What is not deleted

  • Workouts and settings stored on your phone. To remove them, uninstall RepRise or clear its storage in Android settings.
  • Technical logs that contain no workout data expire on their own within 30 days.
