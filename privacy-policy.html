RepRise

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

RepRise is a workout tracking app made by an independent developer. This page explains what the app stores, what happens if you choose to back up to the cloud, and how to delete your data. Questions: keerthivarma2507@gmail.com.

1. What stays on your phone

RepRise stores your workouts on your phone: exercises, sets, weights, repetitions, effort ratings, rest times and app settings such as your appearance choice. If you choose to enter them, it also stores your weight, height, age, sex, activity level and goal, and works out your BMI and daily nutrition numbers on the phone. All of this stays on your phone unless you turn on cloud backup. RepRise works without an account and without an internet connection. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data erases it, unless you have a backup.

2. Optional account and cloud backup

You can create an account with your email address and a password to keep a backup of your data. This is optional.

  • Sign-in is provided by Amazon Cognito (Amazon Web Services). Your password is handled by Amazon and is never visible to the developer. Verification and password-reset emails are sent through Amazon.
  • Backup copy. When you back up, a copy of your workout data and settings (including your body details, if you entered them) is sent to Amazon Web Services and stored in Amazon S3 in the Asia Pacific (Sydney, Australia) region, in a folder linked to your account ID. It is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and encrypted at rest. Only your signed-in account can read or change that folder.
  • Older copies. Previous versions of your backup are kept for up to 30 days so that a failed upload cannot overwrite a good backup, then removed.
  • Signing out removes your sign-in from the phone. It does not delete your workouts.

3. What RepRise does not do

  • It shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics tools.
  • It does not sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

4. Service providers

Amazon Web Services (Cognito, API Gateway, Lambda, S3 and CloudWatch) processes data on the developer's behalf to provide sign-in and backup. Short-lived technical logs, which do not contain your workout data, are kept for up to 30 days to fix problems.

5. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete your account in the app: Profile, then Backup & Account, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and every cloud backup copy. Workouts on your phone are not deleted.
  • Delete without the app: see how to request deletion.
  • Delete data on your phone: uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.
  • Access and correction: you can see and edit your data in the app, and export it as text from Backup & Account.

6. Health information

BMI, calorie, protein, carbohydrate, fat, vitamin and strength-progress figures in RepRise are general estimates for adults and are not medical advice. They do not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Talk to a doctor or dietitian before changing your diet or training, especially if you are pregnant, have a medical condition or take medication.

7. Children

RepRise is not directed at children under 13, and it gives no calorie or weight targets to anyone under 18.

8. Security and retention

Data on your phone stays until you delete it. Cloud data stays until you delete your account. Sign-in tokens on your phone are kept in Android's secure storage. No system is perfectly secure, but RepRise limits what it collects to what it needs.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change and the app will point to the new version.
